## Monetization Models

- **Affiliate** — Product recommendation articles with affiliate links
- **Sponsored** — Paid placements from SaaS/B2B companies
- **Lead Gen** — Capture leads for insurance/legal/finance partners
- **Newsletter** — Drive email signups for future monetization
- **AdSense** — Display ads on high-traffic informational content
- **Course Affiliate** — Promote online courses for commission

## Content Guidelines

1. Every article must have real sources and fact-checking for YMYL topics
2. Affiliate disclosures must be present on all commercial articles
3. No keyword stuffing — write for humans first
4. Minimum 1500 words for comparison/review articles
5. Include comparison tables, pros/cons, and clear recommendations
6. Update articles quarterly to maintain freshness
7. Trust score must be 70+ before publishing YMYL content
