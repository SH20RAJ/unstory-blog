## Notes

- All YMYL articles (finance, insurance, legal, health) must have:
  - Fact-check status of "verified" or "reviewed"
  - At least one real source
  - Appropriate disclaimers
  - Trust score >= 70
- Never publish financial advice — use "informational/educational only" framing
- Every affiliate link must have proper disclosure
- Comparison pages must be based on real testing/research
