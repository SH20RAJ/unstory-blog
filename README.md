# Unstory.app

**Unstory.app** is a premium content platform built around one idea:

> Rich people do not just consume entertainment. They consume leverage, opportunity, status, risk, money, health, power, and taste.

Unstory.app curates and publishes high-value internet categories that attract affluent audiences, high-intent buyers, founders, investors, executives, professionals, and the middle-class audience with a rich mindset.

The platform is not built for random viral traffic. It is built for **high-value attention**.

---

## Positioning

Unstory.app is a modern digital publication for people who want to understand:

* where money is moving,
* what smart people are buying,
* what rich people are watching,
* what ambitious people are learning,
* what technologies are changing industries,
* and what trends can create wealth, power, status, freedom, or influence.

The content style should feel like a mix of:

* premium business newsletter,
* tech intelligence brief,
* luxury magazine,
* investment research note,
* founder/operator playbook,
* and cultural trend radar.

## Content Principle

Every category on Unstory.app should answer at least one of these questions:

1. **Can this help someone make money?**
2. **Can this help someone save money?**
3. **Can this help someone save time?**
4. **Can this increase status?**
5. **Can this reduce risk?**
6. **Can this improve health, performance, or longevity?**
7. **Can this help someone become more powerful, skilled, or informed?**
8. **Can this influence a large financial decision?**

If the answer is no to all of these, the topic is probably not a core Unstory topic.

# Editorial Formats

## 1. Briefs

Short posts for fast updates.

Example:

> OpenAI launches a new tool for enterprise customers. Here is what it means for startups, workers, and investors.

## 2. Explainers

Simple explanations of complex topics.

Example:

> AI Regulation Explained for Founders

## 3. Lists

High-intent SEO and affiliate format.

Example:

> 15 AI Tools Every Founder Should Try

## 4. Comparisons

Great for affiliate and buying intent.

Example:

> Best Premium Credit Cards for Entrepreneurs

## 5. Breakdowns

Deep analysis of companies, trends, and people.

Example:

> How Nvidia Became the Most Important Company in AI

## 6. Playbooks

Actionable guides.

Example:

> How to Build a Personal AI Workflow

## 7. Watchlists

Recurring premium content.

Example:

> 10 Trends Smart Money Is Watching This Month

## 8. Case Studies

High-value business content.

Example:

> How a Boring Software Company Became a $100M Business

## 9. Opinion with Evidence

Strong perspective, but grounded in facts.

Example:

> The Middle Class Is Not Poor Because of Income Alone. It Is Poor Because of Operating Systems.

Long-form monetizable assets.

# Content Scoring System

Every article should be scored before publishing.

## Score each topic from 1 to 5

```txt
Audience Value: Will rich/ambitious people care?
Commercial Value: Can this monetize?
Search Value: Will people search for it?
Trend Value: Is this timely?
Evergreen Value: Will it stay useful?
Brand Value: Does this make Unstory look premium?
Risk Level: Could this cause legal, financial, medical, or political issues?
```

Prioritize content with:

```txt
Audience Value >= 4
Commercial Value >= 3
Brand Value >= 4
Risk Level <= 3
```

For politics, health, legal, and investing, risk review is mandatory.

* fake guru language,

# Editorial Safety Rules

## Finance

* No guaranteed returns.
* No personalized investment advice.
* Always separate education from recommendation.

## Health

* No diagnosis.
* No medical certainty.
* Cite qualified sources when making health claims.

## Legal

* No legal advice.
* Use jurisdiction disclaimers.
* Recommend professional consultation.

## Politics

* Avoid misinformation.
* Avoid hate or targeting.
* Avoid rage bait.
* Separate facts from opinion.
* Use multiple credible sources for sensitive claims.

## Luxury

* Avoid fake scarcity.
* Avoid counterfeit promotion.
* Focus on taste, value, craft, and culture.
